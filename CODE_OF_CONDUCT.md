# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Study Senpai is a small open-source project. Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and careful with privacy-sensitive data.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Discuss ideas and tradeoffs directly without personal attacks.
- Use fake data in issues, pull requests, screenshots, and demos.
- Report security or privacy concerns responsibly.
- Keep reviews focused on the code, docs, and user impact.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private data, secrets, chat logs, or credentials.
- Submitting screenshots or fixtures that contain real private conversations.
- Deliberately bypassing security controls or encouraging unsafe deployment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository's preferred private channel. For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and redact sensitive values as `[REDACTED]`.
